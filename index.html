  • All apostrophe-like marks after o / g are treated the same: oʻ, o‘, o’, o', o` → ö; same for g.
  • Other letters are unchanged. ng is no longer a separate letter but is still written ng.
  • The apostrophe that separates vowels or marks a glottal stop (ma'no, mas'ul) stays and is normalised to ʼ. A split like s'h or c'h is not read as sh / ch.
